IF YOUR IPHONE SAYS “NO INTERNET”

Stay connected anyway

  1. In Wi-Fi settings, select Axiom and choose to keep using the network.
  2. Open Safari and type http://10.10.10.1.
  3. Use http://, not https://. This library is physically local to the Pi.
  4. For one-tap access, use Safari’s Share button and choose Add to Home Screen.
Use sound judgment.

This library is a reference, not a substitute for qualified medical, structural, electrical, or radiation-safety professionals. Confirm critical instructions in more than one reputable source when possible. Never back-feed an electrical grid. Nuclear material is educational and does not make a household reactor or radioisotope generator safe or lawful.
