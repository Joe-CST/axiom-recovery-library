The difference is the number of large searchable archives downloaded after the
first boot.

## 256 GB: essential and practical

This is the recommended budget edition. It keeps illustrated Wikipedia, maps,
all practical manuals, medical material, agriculture, appropriate technology,
food, shelter, repair, power, engineering, communications, navigation, and the
smaller practical Q&A archives.

It removes five expansion collections: Wiktionary, Wikiversity, Survivor
Library, Khan Academy, and Project Gutenberg.

## 512 GB: deeper practical archive

This edition adds Wiktionary, Wikiversity, and the large historical Survivor
Library. It is a strong choice when historical technical books and a larger
education collection matter more than lowest cost.

It removes Khan Academy and Project Gutenberg.

## 1 TB: complete collection

This edition includes every configured collection. It adds Khan Academy and
Project Gutenberg to the 512 GB content.

## A note about estimates

Kiwix and map publishers update their archives. The exact size may change after
this guide is published. Every edition checks space before downloading each
archive and maintains a capacity-specific reserve. Optional items can fail or
be skipped without deleting completed downloads.
